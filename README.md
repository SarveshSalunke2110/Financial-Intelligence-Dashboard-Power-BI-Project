
📊 Financial Intelligence Dashboard – Power BI Project

Welcome to my **Financial Intelligence Dashboard** project! This repository showcases an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze financial performance, sales trends, profitability, and product performance. The project transforms financial data into meaningful insights to support data-driven business decisions.

🚀 About This Project

The Financial Intelligence Dashboard provides an executive overview of sales, profit, profit margins, units sold, country-wise performance, and product sales. It helps users monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and evaluate business performance through interactive visualizations.

📈 Key Features

- 💰 **Sales Performance Analysis** – Analyze total sales and compare performance across countries, products, and business segments.
- 📊 **Profitability Analysis** – Monitor total profit and profit margin to understand business profitability.
- 🌍 **Country-wise Sales Analysis** – Identify top-performing countries and compare their contribution to total sales.
- 🏆 **Product Performance Analysis** – Identify top-selling products and analyze sales contribution.
- 🔄 **Financial Flow Analysis** – Visualize the flow from Gross Sales through Discounts, Net Sales, COGS, and Profit.
- 📅 **Monthly Trend Analysis** – Compare monthly sales and profit performance over time.
- 🎯 **KPI Cards** – Display important business metrics in a clear executive summary.
- 🎛️ **Interactive Slicers** – Filter dashboard insights by Year, Country, Segment, Product, and Discount Band.

🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor overall financial and sales performance.
- Identify profitable products and high-performing markets.
- Understand the impact of discounts and cost of goods sold on profitability.
- Track monthly sales, profit, and profit margin trends.
- Present business insights through an interactive executive dashboard.
- Support data-driven decision-making through effective visualization.
